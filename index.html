How do I open the Anki deck after I download it?

The deck is delivered as a .apkg file — it won't open on its own. It needs to be imported into the Anki app first. Once imported, all your cards will be ready to study. The import takes about 30 seconds. Follow whichever step fits your setup below. Or watch the 5-minute walkthrough: YouTube Link

I have an Android phone. What do I need?

Download AnkiDroid for free from the Google Play Store. Open the app, tap the menu, select Import, and choose the .apkg file you downloaded. That's it.

I have an iPhone and a laptop. What do I need?

No extra cost required. Download the free Anki desktop app at apps.ankiweb.net on your laptop. Open the .apkg file in the desktop app to import it. Then create a free account at ankiweb.net, click Sync in the desktop app, and your cards will be available at ankiweb.net in your iPhone's browser at no extra cost.

I only have an iPhone — no laptop. What do I need?

Unfortunately this is the one scenario that requires an additional purchase. You'll need AnkiMobile ($24.99 one-time) from the App Store. This is an Apple limitation — AnkiMobile is the only way to import a deck directly on iPhone without a computer. Once installed, tap the .apkg file and it will import automatically.

I'm getting "failed to load" when I try to import the deck. What do I do?

This almost always means you're using AnkiPro, not the real Anki. AnkiPro is a different app from a different company, has a paid subscription, and can't reliably import .apkg files. Real Anki has no subscription. To fix it, follow whichever scenario above matches your setup (Android, iPhone + laptop, or iPhone only) using the actual Anki and re-import the deck. If you've confirmed you're on real Anki and still see the error, your download probably didn't complete — re-download the .apkg and try again.
